# Life Wrapped™ — by God

A Spotify-Wrapped-style year-in-review, except the reviewer is God and the
year is your entire existence. Dark humor, big numbers, no refunds.

Open `index.html` in any browser — the whole app is one self-contained file,
no build step, no network, nothing stored.

## What's inside

- **Two preset wraps**, fully written and fact-grounded where facts exist:
  - **Elon Musk** — minutes on the app he bought, Mars ETA (5 years away for
    the 14th consecutive year), the camel/needle situation.
  - **Fyodor Dostoevsky** — his most-quoted line on the internet ("Above all,
    don't lie to yourself," *The Brothers Karamazov*, 30k+ Goodreads likes),
    his most used word ("suddenly," ~560× in *Crime and Punishment* per
    Toporov's count), the 26-day *Gambler* speedrun, and one word for people
    who relate to him: *Unwell.*
- **Wrap yourself**: enter a name/@handle, optionally paste your tweets/bio/
  3am notes. The app analyzes the text client-side (most used word, main
  character index from self-reference rate) and generates a wrap. Pick God's
  mood: **Self-Aware Spiral** (BoJack-core), **Roast Mode** (Samay-core), or
  **Old Testament** (smite-adjacent deadpan).

## Interactions (story format)

- Tap right/left to advance/go back, **hold to pause** — Instagram-stories
  muscle memory. Segmented progress bar up top. Auto-advance per card.
- Count-up stats, staggered top-5 slams, suspense-dots personality reveal
  with confetti, an itemized "receipts" card, synthesized pops and fanfares
  (Web Audio, no files).
- Final card is a screenshot-ready summary with **Save card ↓** — renders a
  1080×1920 share PNG on a canvas and downloads it.

Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Everything runs locally; nothing is sent
anywhere. Parody — not affiliated with any streaming service or deity.
