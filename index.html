About this machine

The Westward Museum's Art Rewind is a time machine for Western art. Hold the rewind control and nineteen millennia spool backwards — from the glow of your screen to the glow of a torch on a limestone wall. Release, and you step into a gallery room dressed in the palette, the frames, and the light of that moment.

Every painting in the collection is an original study rendered live in code — spray-stencilled, gilded, dabbed, dripped, or pixel-sorted in the technique of its era. Click any work to read its wall label.

Interaction pattern inspired by Opera's «Web Rewind» internet time machine · All artworks generated in-browser · No two visits paint exactly alike
